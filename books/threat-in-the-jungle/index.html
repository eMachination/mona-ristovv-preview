About the book

Threat in the Jungle: Escaping Danger

High school environmentalist Elena Ramirez visits a Costa Rican jungle and encounters a long-buried mystery involving the rainforest, illegal activity, and danger.

The opening installment brings young investigators, a strong sense of place, environmental concerns, and a mystery that grows larger as Elena keeps asking questions. The rainforest is part of what she is trying to understand and protect.
