Author • Educator • Researcher

A broad professional life behind an unusually broad body of work.

Dr. Mona Ristovv Reed, publishing fiction primarily as Mona Ristovv, is an author, educator, researcher, instructional designer, and grant writer. Her experience spans higher education, behavioral science, educational technology, technical writing, grant development, and interdisciplinary research.

Her books include the twelve-book Elena & Diego: High School Detectives series, memoir, homecraft and DIY, Cajun children’s literature, educational publications, collaborative student books, and scholarly research.
