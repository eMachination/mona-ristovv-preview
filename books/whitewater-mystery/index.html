About the book

The Whitewater Mystery

A whitewater rafting trip on Costa Rica’s Balsa River turns dangerous when Elena and Diego are drawn into a criminal caper connected with a treasure chest.

The investigation unfolds in an active river environment, giving this mystery a sense of movement and immediate risk.
