About the book

Tilled and Taken: An Elena and Diego Adventure

Elena and Diego leave Costa Rica to present research on an engineered agricultural pathogen and discover that the threat has reached the American food supply.

The twelfth installment expands the series geographically, turning a scientific warning into a race through agricultural and food-production infrastructure.
